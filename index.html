HYROX Season 9: fastest 1000m Row splits

Top 3 row times per race and category for Season 9 (labelled "Season 26/27" on the official site), compiled from the public results lists. Data as of .

    Where the data comes from

    • Row split lists: results.hyrox.com/season-9, one list per race day, ranked by the 1000m Row split.
    • Race calendar (dates, venues, live and upcoming status): event pages on hyresult.com, an independent community site. It has no per-station race rankings, so it was used for the calendar only.
    • Categories: Men / Women are the open "HYROX" single-day lists, merged across race days. Men Pro / Women Pro are the "HYROX PRO" lists. Doubles, Team Relay, Adaptive and Youngstars are not included.
    • Times are published to the whole second, so ties are common. Order inside a tie follows the official list within a day and is arbitrary across days. A ‡ marks a time tied with further athletes at the 3rd/4th cut-off.
    • Six races were mid-weekend on the data date (Barcelona, Düsseldorf, Dublin, Denver, Seoul, Cairo), so their lists are interim and will change. Cairo has no results yet.

    Cleaning of timing glitches

    The raw official Row ranking contains impossible values (for example 0:21, 0:28 or 2:15 for 1000 m) caused by missed timing-mat reads. Taking the raw fastest times would put those athletes first, so every candidate was validated:

    1. Plausibility floor: a Row faster than 3:00 (men) or 3:20 (women) is rejected.
    2. Consistency: a Row faster than 75% of the athlete's own 1000m SkiErg time is rejected. All glitches inspected failed this test, while genuine elite results passed.
    3. Faulty days: a whole race day is ignored if the median of its 25 fastest Row splits is under 80% of the typical day. Only Maastricht on Saturday (men) qualified: median 2:50 against a typical 4:18.

    These thresholds are my judgement, not an official standard. A glitch that only trims a few seconds cannot be detected.

    Finishers only

    Athletes without a finish time on record (DNF or partial results) are not eligible, so every top 3 consists of finishers only. This changed three Men Pro lists: Tampa (a 3:12 removed), Rome (3:52 removed) and Chiba (3:55 removed); the next finishers moved up.
